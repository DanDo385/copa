**Duration:** ~20 minutes

## What we built

A **Shopify-ready storefront prototype** that shows how COPA's live-commerce trust translates into an owned website — complete catalogue, education, gifting, and repeat purchase paths — without touching the live store.

- Brazilian modern editorial design (warm stone, sea teal, gold)
- Every required route mapped (shop, categories, PDP, cart, live shows, policies)
- Claim and placeholder discipline — nothing false presented as fact
- Migration map to Shopify Online Store 2.0

## Why this matters

| Today | With this direction |
|-------|---------------------|
| Strongest proof lives on Whatnot, not the homepage | Whatnot metrics attributed on-site with clear platform context |
| Contradictory shipping, returns, warranty | One canonical policy structure — awaiting your decisions |
| 330 products, weak filters | Category, finish, material, stone, price, availability filters |
| Generic catalog feel | Founders, styling notes, "Complete the look" merchandising |
| Broken cart gift snippet on live site | Clean cart UX spec without Liquid errors |
| Ads blocked by policy gaps | Clear 15-gate checklist — honest **BLOCKED** status |

Critical first decisions:

1. **Brand name** — COPA Jewelry vs legal entity display
2. **Pricing** — retire permanent 25% off?
3. **Returns** — refund or store credit? how many days?
4. **Shipping** — processing time, free threshold, international
5. **Warranty** — what "lifetime" actually means
6. **Contact** — one email, hours, return address
7. **Founder bios** — what you want public
8. **Product specs** — supplier sheets for top SKUs

Full register: `docs/owner-decision-register.md`

## What blocks ads (honest)

**All 15 ad-readiness gates: FAIL or NEEDS DATA. Overall: BLOCKED.**

We will not recommend Google Ads spend until policies, tracking, feed, inventory, and unit economics are verified. The prototype is a storefront prerequisite, not a launch authorization.
